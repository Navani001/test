8. Proposed Hybrid OCR Approach
The evaluation indicates that Indra OCR provides good text extraction performance and inference time on commonly encountered medical record pages. However, its performance decreases on complex pages such as handwritten, blurry, oriented, packed, and image-heavy pages.
Based on these observations, Indra OCR can be used as the primary OCR engine for normal pages, while alternative OCR engines such as AWS Textract or Baidu Unlimited-OCR can be considered for conflict pages.
8.1 Proposed Workflow
The proposed approach involves processing documents at the page level:
Document Input: Receive the input PDF.
Page Separation: Split the document into individual pages.
Page Classification: Classify pages into normal pages and conflict pages.
OCR Routing: Route normal pages to Indra OCR and conflict pages to a suitable alternative OCR engine.
Output Merging: Combine the extracted results while preserving the original page order.
8.2 Further Investigation
A reliable page-level classification mechanism is required to distinguish normal pages from conflict pages. Further investigation is needed to identify suitable classification features, evaluate classification methods, and select appropriate alternative OCR engines for different conflict-page categories.
The proposed hybrid approach has not yet been implemented or validated. Its effectiveness must be evaluated through further experiments.
9. Summary
The evaluation was conducted to assess Indra OCR as a potential OCR engine for medical record processing and to understand its suitability as an alternative or complement to AWS Textract.
Indra OCR demonstrated good text extraction performance on the 100-page dataset containing commonly encountered medical records. It also showed good inference performance, indicating its suitability for processing regular document pages.
However, evaluation on the diversified 1,000-page dataset revealed limitations in handling certain complex document types, including oriented, handwritten, blurry, image-heavy, and packed pages. The analysis also identified specific extraction issues, such as character recognition errors and word splitting in documents using Courier New font.
Based on these observations, Indra OCR is suitable for commonly encountered medical record pages but may not consistently handle all complex document types. A hybrid OCR approach is proposed in which Indra OCR processes normal pages and alternative OCR engines handle conflict pages.
The major requirement for implementing this approach is a reliable page-level classification mechanism to distinguish normal pages from conflict pages. Further investigation and experimentation are required to develop this mechanism, identify suitable alternative OCR engines, and validate the overall hybrid system.
